# Privacy Policy – YouTube Playlist Sorter

Last updated: September 2026

This extension does not collect, sell, or share personal information.

**What the extension does with data**
- To sort playlists by views, duration, or upload date, the extension sends the IDs of the YouTube videos on the page to a proxy server hosted on Vercel (yt-organizer-sage.vercel.app). The proxy asks the YouTube Data API for public video information and returns it. No account details, browsing history, or personal data are sent, and the proxy does not store the video IDs.
- Videos you save, notes, and settings are stored locally in your browser using the extension's storage. They are not sent anywhere else.
- Thumbnail downloads and CSV exports happen on your device.

**Third parties**
Public video data is provided by the YouTube API (Google). See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
